# Crystal Grove

A walk-around game for the [wedgie](https://wedgie.dev). A knight with a sword stands in the middle of
the screen and a big world slides under him: grass, stone paths, ponds, trees, rocks and bushes.
Slash a bush and, one time in five, there's a crystal under it. Walk over a crystal to pick it up.

The world is the same every time (96 x 96 tiles, the sea all round it). The crystals aren't.

## Controls

- Joystick: walk.
- **A** (green): slash the bush in front of you.

## Save

`crystals`: how many you hold (a number). In the emulator (wedgie.dev/code) edit it, or delete it to
start from 0.

## Play it

- Emulator: https://wedgie.dev/code, Open a folder (this one), or load `clawdbotatg/wedgie-grove`.
- A wedgie: `python3 wedgie.py install .` ([wedgie.py](https://wedgie.dev/wedgie.py)).
